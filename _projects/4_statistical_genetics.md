My PhD research at Columbia developed statistical methods for human genetics.

- **Heterogeneity in GWAS cohorts.** I showed how correlations between variants in polygenic risk scores can reveal hidden heterogeneity among cases in genome-wide association cohorts, and applied the method to schizophrenia data with the Psychiatric Genomics Consortium. {% cite yuan2020leveraging %}
- **Reverse TWAS.** Reversing the direction of transcriptome-wide association studies improves the discovery of expression quantitative trait loci. {% cite yuan2020reversing %}
