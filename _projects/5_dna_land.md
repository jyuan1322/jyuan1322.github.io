DNA.Land was a framework for collecting genomes and phenomes from people who already had direct-to-consumer genetic data, so they could contribute to research. The site has since been discontinued. I'm first author on the [paper describing the platform](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-017-0021-8). {% cite yuan2018dnaland %}
