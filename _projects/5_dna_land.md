[DNA.Land](https://dna.land) is a framework that collects genomes and phenomes from people who already have direct-to-consumer genetic data, so they can contribute to research. I'm first author on the paper describing the platform. {% cite yuan2018dnaland %}

I presented a massively scalable approach to phenotyping through social media as a platform talk at ASHG 2016. In the Erlich lab, I also coauthored work on genome-wide profiling of heritable and _de novo_ STR variation. {% cite willems2017str %}
