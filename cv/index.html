Professional Summary

Machine learning scientist with a PhD in computer science and 5+ years of experience designing and implementing advanced statistical models and computational pipelines for large-scale biological datasets. Specializes in Bayesian modeling, spatial omics, multi-omics integration, next-generation sequencing pipelines, microbiome analysis, NMR data processing and analysis, and ML engineering. Broadly interested in machine learning, statistical genomics, genome-wide association, problems in spatial analysis and geometry, and interactive data visualization.

Experience

  • 2025 - 2026
    Machine Learning Scientist
    Brigham and Women's Hospital / Harvard Medical School
    Advisor: Georg Gerber
    • Built preprocessing, calibration, and Bayesian (Stan) modeling pipelines to characterize C. difficile metabolic dynamics (dFBA, COBRApy) from real-time NMR spectroscopy data, with uncertainty propagated into flux estimates, cutting analysis time from 2 weeks to 6 hours.
    • Built reproducible multi-omics pipelines for microbiome cohorts (16S, shotgun metagenomics, NMR metabolomics, proteomics) parallelized on a SLURM cluster, and a Python ML framework with shared modeling code across assays and dynamic handling of clinical covariates.
    • Provided metagenomic and 16S microbiome data analysis and consultation for external collaborating labs from raw sequencing data (DADA2, phyloseq, MetaPhlAn, HUMAnN), and presented statistical findings to non-technical audiences.
  • 2021 - 2025
    Postdoctoral Research Fellow → Research Scientist
    Brigham and Women's Hospital / Harvard Medical School → Yale School of Medicine (moved with lab, July 2024)
    Advisors: Xianjun Dong and Clemens Scherzer
    • Led computational development of a multi-omic spatial and temporal cell atlas of Parkinson’s disease pathology (manuscript in preparation), integrating spatial transcriptomic and single-cell data across disease stages. Analyses included annotation (clustering, batch correction, dimensionality reduction), differential expression (various regression methods), and single-cell/spatial deconvolution for data integration.
    • Developed a machine learning tool to infer nonlinear boundaries between brain cortical layers from highly noisy 10x Visium spatial data, solving a previously manual and error-prone analytical bottleneck.
    • Built a web application for manual selection and filtering of spots falling under spatial artifacts, streamlining a previously manual QC step.
    • Implemented variational graph autoencoder-based integration methods for hexagonally arranged spatial data to build a joint model across heterogeneous omics data types.
    • Conducted Gaussian process time-series analysis of bulk blood gene expression in ~600 Parkinson’s patients across longitudinal clinical visits using Stan, as part of the AMP-PD consortium.
    • Managed lab compute infrastructure, including server administration, HPC job scheduling, and IT support.
